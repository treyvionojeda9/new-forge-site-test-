How much does a website from Forge Systems cost?+
All-in website package: $149/mo with a 12-month commitment, or $960 paid for the year.
Do I have to pay a deposit?+
No. You pay only once your site is live on your domain.
What is the free preview?+
It is a private version of your new website, built with your real public business information, before anyone pitches you anything. Nobody else can see it.
Is the preview really free?+
Yes. Look at it. If it is not for you, no hard feelings.
What is AEO?+
AEO means answer engine optimization: setting up your site so AI assistants like ChatGPT and Google's AI answers can find and quote your business. We do it with FAQs written as real questions, clear facts about your services and area, and structured data.
Can you guarantee my business will rank #1 on Google?+
No. Nobody honest can. We guarantee professional execution, not algorithm outcomes: fast pages, correct local details and clean structured data.
What is included in the all-in website package?+
The website, hosting and care, Google Business Profile setup, Yelp setup, and a weekly article. Your website includes up to 5 pages, mobile-first design, built to load fast on phones, call and contact buttons, a Google Maps embed and a full SEO and AEO launch checklist.
What does $149 a month include?+
Your website, hosting and care, your Google Business Profile, your Yelp page, and a new article every week. Or pay $960 for the year.
What are custom business systems?+
They are back-office setups like a CRM, lead follow-up automations, online booking and an AI receptionist. Each one is quoted per client after we see what you actually need.
